Getting Better

Tips

  1. Improve Reaction Time

    2. In a video game, reaction time is the amount of time that elapses between an event and the gamer's response. It's a critical component of gaming, especially in fast-paced action games like first-person shooter (FPS) games, racing games, and fighting games. For example, the faster you react in an FPS game, the better you'll perform in competition when dodging attacks or shooting an enemy. It's important to train your reaction time so you'll have no problems reacting to a situation.

  2. Stay Calm

    3. Staying calm allows us to think logically and make decisions accordingly. More so, being calm helps you get a more relaxed and free mind to think over the tension rising at that particular time; giving you more time to think logically and come up with a solution to that particular problem. If you aren't calm in a fast-pased situation, wether it's a first-person shooter game or a fighting game, you'll make decisions that could affect the play drastically because you didn't think rationally.

  3. Improve Your Senses

    4. Not all your five senses will be used: taste, smell, and touch. Improving your vision and hearing will give you a massive advantage boost to your competitors. Vision can help you by seeing what is happening in that certian period of time. If you have a fast reaction time, what's the point of it if you couldn't see the situation at hand anyways. Hearing is also very important in certian genres like first-person shooters. It can help you differentiate the sound of players and the game. It can help inform players what may be coming after them.

Performance

  • Frames Per Second

    • Low FPS implies that the game is struggling to render enough frames each second, resulting in choppy or laggy gameplay. Low FPS can make your games look choppy, blurry, and laggy.It can also increase input lag, which can make your games feel sluggish and unresponsive. Low FPS can cause stuttering, which is a sudden drop or spike in FPS that can disrupt your gameplay and immersion. On the other hand, high FPS signifies that your computer has no problem rendering every frame quickly, resulting in a smoother gaming experience overall.

    What affects your FPS?
    Graphics Card, CPU, and RAM.

  • Ping

    • Ping means the time it takes for a small data set to be transmitted from your device to a server on the Internet and back to your device again. High ping results in a disconnect between your button presses and the actions that happen in the game. The higher the ping, the worse this becomes.

    What improves your ping?
    Upgrading your router, using an ethernet cable, or adjusting your frame rate.
